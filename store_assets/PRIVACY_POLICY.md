ResumePace ("we", "us", our) is a mobile application for building, optimizing
and exporting resumes. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it,
and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** When you sign up or sign in we collect your email
address. Authentication is handled by Firebase Auth; we do not store your
password.

**Resume content.** The resumes you create — including your name, contact
details, work history, education, skills and summaries — are stored in your
account (Cloud Firestore) so they sync across your devices. PDF and DOCX export
sends the selected resume to a Firebase Cloud Function that renders the file.

**AI processing.** When you use AI features (summary generation, bullet
improvement, skill suggestions, CV optimization) your resume content is sent to
OpenAI's API to produce the response. This content is used only to fulfil your
request and is not used to train models by us.

**In-app purchases.** Purchases of optimization credits are processed by Apple
(App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your full payment
card details. We store only the number of credits remaining on your account.

**Advertising.** The app contains advertising. Google AdMob collects a device
identifier (such as the Advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP,
and interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required (including the
EEA and UK) an consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can
withdraw consent at any time from your device settings or Google's
[Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

**Technical information.** Basic diagnostics such as network status
(connectivity check) and error logs are processed to keep the app working.

## Information we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information. Resume content is never shared with
advertisers.

## How we use information

- To provide, sync and back up your resumes
- To deliver the AI features you explicitly request
- To process and restore your purchases
- To display and measure advertising, including personalised ads where consented
- To respond to support requests

## Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

- **Contract performance** for account, sync and export features
- **Legitimate interests** for app security, diagnostics and non-personalised ads
- **Consent** for personalised advertising and any optional processing

## Data retention

Resume data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account removes
your resumes, profile and remaining credits from our systems. Advertising
identifiers are managed by Google under its own retention terms.

## Children

ResumePace is a professional productivity tool and is not directed at children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you
believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Depending on your location you may request access to, correction of, or
deletion of your personal data, and may object to or restrict certain
processing. Email support@nextfictiongames.com and we will respond within 30 days.
